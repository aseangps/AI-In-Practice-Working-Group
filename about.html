About the Working Group

Learn more about our mission, structure, and how to participate

What is a Working Group?

A Working Group (WG) is like a Study Group, but instead of preparing to pass an exam, participants focus on discussions and activities around a specific subject area. Think of it like an extended Hackathon, where a Hackathon focuses on intense collaboration within a day or two, the Working Group collaborates over 5 two-hour sessions, spread out over 5 months.

Wikipedia Working Group definition: A group of experts working together to achieve specified goals. Such groups are domain-specific and focus on discussion or activity around a specific subject area. The term can sometimes refer to an interdisciplinary collaboration of researchers, often from more than one organization, working on new activities that would be difficult to sustain under traditional funding mechanisms.

Our Mission

The AI-In-Practice Working Group aims to co-develop publicly shared references (code / best-practices) on practical applications of AI for areas such as:

  • Productivity: Enhancing workflows and efficiency
  • Security: AI-powered security solutions and reviews
  • Governance: Ethical AI implementation and oversight
  • Sustainability: Environmentally conscious AI practices
  • Culture: Building inclusive AI-enabled communities

We emphasize collaborative learning (learning by doing) and learning in public.

Ground Rules

🤝 Pro-active Participation

Participate in ideation, research & discussions whenever possible

⚖️ Ethical Principles

Adhere to fairness, transparency, accountability and respect for user privacy

📈 Continuous Learning

Encourage continuous learning and improvement

🔄 Early Pivoting

Topic changes before the 3rd meeting are OK based on group consensus

🌟 Respectful Environment

Foster an environment where all participants feel comfortable to share

How We Work

Central to our communication structure is the Ideas board which comprises of 3 pools representing each stage of a work/learn/research item:

💡 Add New Ideas

Ideas can be added to the Ideas pool. Ideas need not be assigned to anyone in particular. The idea creator does not necessarily need to work on the idea.

🔬 Work on an Idea

One or more participants can pick up an idea to research. Move the idea from the Ideas Pool to the Research Pool to let the group know it's being worked on.

💬 Discuss Ideas

Ideas can be discussed asynchronously on Discord or GitHub. Participants are encouraged to fork the repository, add notes, and create pull requests for discussion.

📢 Present Research

When ideas are sufficiently researched, they can be presented at the monthly meetup. Once presented, the item moves to the Presented pool.
